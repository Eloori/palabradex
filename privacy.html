Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Summary: We respect your privacy. Without an account, Palabradex runs entirely in your browser and your learning progress is stored only locally on your device. If you choose to create an optional account, your email address and your learning progress are stored with our provider Supabase so that you can use the same progress on all your devices. You can delete your account at any time in the app. There is no tracking, no analytics and no advertising.

1. Applicable Law

This privacy policy is governed by the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP). Where the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, its requirements are also met.

2. Data Controller

Responsible for data processing on this website is:
Elija Zemp, Zylistrasse 10, 9000 St.Gallen, Switzerland.
For any privacy-related inquiries, you can contact contact@eloori-interactive.com.

3. Hosting & Server Log Files (GitHub Pages)

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P Kelly Jr St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). GitHub is the technical recipient of your data to enable the delivery of the website.

When you access our website, the web server (GitHub) automatically collects technical information in so-called server log files. This is technically necessary to display the website and to ensure stability and security. This data includes:

  • IP address of the requesting device
  • Date and time of access
  • Name and URL of the retrieved file
  • Website from which access is made (referrer URL)
  • Browser and operating system used

Purpose: Processing is carried out to ensure network security and error analysis.

Legal basis: Legitimate interest in the secure and stable operation of the website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; Art. 31(1) FADP).

Data transfer to the USA: Since GitHub is based in the USA, data may be transferred to the USA. The transfer is based on the provider's applicable safeguards (including Standard Contractual Clauses and/or certification under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework).

Storage duration: Log files are temporarily stored according to GitHub's guidelines and are generally deleted automatically after a few days.

Right to object: Since the collection of this data is technically mandatory for the provision and security of the website, users have no possibility to object.

4. Learning Progress (Local Storage)

Palabradex saves your learning progress (collected words, completed chapters, quiz results, settings such as font size and translation language) in the local storage of your browser. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to us or to third parties.

If you install Palabradex as an app or use it offline, your browser additionally keeps a copy of the app files (offline cache) on your device. This cache contains no personal data.

You can delete this data at any time via Settings → Reset progress or by clearing your browser data. The optional export function, the PDF albums and the share images are created directly in your browser on your own device; we do not receive them.

5. Optional User Account (Supabase)

You can use Palabradex without an account. If you choose to sign in, you can do so with your email address (you receive a one-time code by email) or with your Google account. The account is used only to keep your learning progress in sync across your devices.

Provider: Accounts and synced progress are managed by Supabase (Supabase Inc., USA), which acts as our processor. The data is stored on servers in the region selected for our project.

Data processed: your email address, a user ID, the time of sign-in and your learning progress (the same data described in section 4). When you sign in with Google, Google tells us your email address and name. We do not receive your Google password. Google processes your sign-in according to its own privacy policy.

Purpose and legal basis: Providing the account and syncing your progress at your request (performance of a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; Art. 31(1) FADP).

Data transfer to the USA: Supabase and Google are based in the USA. Transfers are based on the providers’ safeguards (including Standard Contractual Clauses and/or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework).

Storage duration and deletion: Your account data is stored until you delete your account. You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and your synced progress from the server. You can also ask us by email to delete it.

6. Cookies & Tracking

We do not use tracking cookies, tracking, analytics (such as Google Analytics) or advertising on this website.

7. Fonts

All fonts are hosted on this website itself. No connection to external font services (such as Google Fonts) is established.

8. Read-Aloud Function

The pronunciation feature uses the speech synthesis built into your browser or operating system. Depending on your browser and the selected voice, the provider of your browser or operating system (e.g. Google, Apple or Microsoft) may process the text that is read aloud according to its own privacy policy. We do not receive this data. If you do not want this, simply do not use the speaker button.

9. Your Rights

Within the scope of the applicable data protection law, you have the right to information, rectification, deletion, restriction of processing and data portability. Without an account, we do not store any personal data about you ourselves (apart from the server log files processed by GitHub). If you have an account, you can delete it and all synced data at any time in the app, and we will answer requests about it by email. For questions, please contact contact@eloori-interactive.com.

10. Changes

We may update this privacy policy if the website or the legal situation changes. The current version is always available on this page.
